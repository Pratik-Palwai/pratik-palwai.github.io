About Me

Hi, I'm Pratik. I am an undergraduate studying Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Information & Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine (graduating June 2028).

I focus on mechanical design, embedded systems, vehicle dynamics, and precision fabrication. Currently, I am an Off-Road Engineering Intern at Hyundai Motor Group, where I work on traction control software for the Santa Fe XRT and calibrate vehicle load sensors using strain gauges.

On campus, I lead aircraft design for UCI UAV Forge, serve as a staff technician at the Samueli Machine Shop, and previously led powertrain engineering for Formula SAE Electric and AIAA Design/Build/Fly.
