UCI UAV Forge SUAS • Aircraft Lead

Custom 36" 4-Axis CNC Hot-Wire Foam Router
UCI AIAA Design/Build/Fly • Chief Engineer

To reduce aircraft structural weight for the AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition, our team needed to cut precision tapered, swept foam wing cores. Hand cutting produced irregular profiles and excessive epoxy weight during composite layups.

I designed and built a 36-inch 4-axis hot-wire CNC machine from scratch. It utilizes independent X/Y/U/V axes controlled by a Raspberry Pi RP2040 microcontroller running a custom-forked grblHAL firmware. I also wrote Python scripts to convert airfoil coordinate files (.dat) into synchronized G-code toolpaths. The resulting cores reduced composite wing structural mass by 40% while exceeding 6G load requirements.

RP2040 grblHAL Firmware C / C++ Composites Layup Vacuum Bagging
Dynamic Propulsion Thrust Stand & Kalman Filter Telemetry
Embedded Systems • Mechatronics Lab

Measuring dynamic thrust on small brushless drone motors is often corrupted by high-frequency motor vibration harmonics that bleed into strain gauge load cell readings.

I built an instrumented cantilever test stand running on an ESP32-C3 microcontroller (programmed via PlatformIO in C++). The firmware implements a real-time discrete 1D Kalman filter to reject mechanical vibration noise while logging current, voltage, and thrust at 100 Hz to generate clean motor/propeller efficiency curves (g/W).
