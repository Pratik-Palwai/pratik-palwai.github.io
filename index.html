About Me

I am an undergraduate studying Mechanical Engineering with a minor in Information / Computer Science at the University of California, Irvine (graduating June 2028).

Previously I have worked on mechanical design, embedded systems, vehicle dynamics, and precision fabrication. The summer after my sophomore year, I was an Off-Road Test/Development Intern at Hyundai, where I developed and tuned traction control software for sand launches on the 2028 Hyundai Santa Fe XRT and built tools to process CANbus data.
