Autonomous Hybrid VTOL UAV (4+1 Quad-Plane)
UCI UAV Forge SUAS • Aircraft Lead

Led all aircraft design and sizing for UCI's first hybrid VTOL UAV (4 vertical lift rotors for VTOL takeoff/landing + 1 pusher propeller for long-range forward flight). The goal was to drastically extend mission radius for the collegiate SUAS search-and-rescue competition.

I wrote first-principles aerodynamic sizing scripts in Python and used Flow5 and MIT xFoil to model airfoil lift/drag polars and Oswald efficiency factors. By optimizing wing area, battery mass fraction, and cruise speed, we increased operational flight range from 11 km to 50 km (+354%) while satisfying payload and stall requirements.

Formula SAE Electric — Powertrain Gearbox & Thermal Control
UCI Formula SAE EV Racing • Powertrain Lead

Responsible for the powertrain gearbox architecture, mechanical drawings, and cooling systems for UCI's Formula SAE electric vehicle. I simulated longitudinal acceleration in Python using basic assumptions of static and kinetic friction and motor torque curves to select the reduction gear ratio, achieving a simulated 0–60 mph launch in under 2.50 seconds.

I drafted 2D manufacturing prints adhering strictly to ASME Y14.5 GD&T standards, machined gearbox hardware on CNC mills, and designed a custom go/no-go inspection fixture to check bearing bore tolerances. Additionally, I implemented closed-loop PID control for coolant pumps and radiator fans to regulate inverter and motor temperatures during endurance tests.

Custom 36" 4-Axis CNC Hot-Wire Foam Router
UCI AIAA Design/Build/Fly • Chief Engineer

To reduce aircraft structural weight for the AIAA Design/Build/Fly competition, our team needed to cut precision tapered, swept foam wing cores. Hand cutting produced irregular profiles and excessive epoxy weight during composite layups.

I designed and built a 36-inch 4-axis hot-wire CNC machine from scratch. It utilizes independent X/Y/U/V axes controlled by a Raspberry Pi RP2040 microcontroller running a custom-forked grblHAL firmware. I also wrote Python scripts to convert airfoil coordinate files (.dat) into synchronized G-code toolpaths. The resulting cores reduced composite wing structural mass by 40% while exceeding 6G load requirements.

RP2040 grblHAL Firmware C / C++ Composites Layup Vacuum Bagging
Dynamic Propulsion Thrust Stand & Kalman Filter Telemetry
Embedded Systems • Mechatronics Lab

Measuring dynamic thrust on small brushless drone motors is often corrupted by high-frequency motor vibration harmonics that bleed into strain gauge load cell readings.

I built an instrumented cantilever test stand running on an ESP32-C3 microcontroller (programmed via PlatformIO in C++). The firmware implements a real-time discrete 1D Kalman filter to reject mechanical vibration noise while logging current, voltage, and thrust at 100 Hz to generate clean motor/propeller efficiency curves (g/W).
